Waqt is a native, fully offline prayer tracking application to help you build a better relationship with your daily prayer.

Struggle with time? Waqt gives you a nudge at just the right time so you don't forget.

If gamification increases your success, Waqt is also perfect for you.

Features:
* Track daily prayer completion with a GitHub contribution graph style streak grid and many other visualization methods
* Waqt and location-based prayer notifications
* Get a reminder right before waqt expires
* Customizable calculation methods and madhab options
* Material You dynamic theming and custom color selection
* Backup and restore prayer history records
* Completely offline with no network permissions required
